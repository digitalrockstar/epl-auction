# Emmanuel Premier League — Season 3
### Rules & Regulations (Draft for Manager/Captain Review)

---

## 1. Tournament Format

- **Format**: T20, IPL-style
- **Teams**: 4
- **League stage**: 6 matches (single round-robin, every team plays every other team once)
- **Playoffs**: Qualifier + Final
- **Overs per innings**: 20
- **Tournament window**: League starts Sept 19, Final on Oct 2

### Playoff structure
- Team ranked **1st** after the league stage goes directly to the Final.
- Teams ranked **2nd and 3rd** play the Qualifier.
- The Qualifier winner plays the 1st-ranked team in the Final.

---

## 2. Points Table

- **Win**: 2 points
- **Loss**: 0 points
- **Tie (scores level after 20 overs)**: Resolved by a Super Over. The Super Over winner is awarded the match win for points purposes.
- **Ranking order**: Points → Net Run Rate (NRR) → Head-to-head result

### Net Run Rate (NRR)
NRR = (Total runs scored ÷ Total overs faced) − (Total runs conceded ÷ Total overs bowled), added up across all matches played, not per match.

- If a team is bowled out (all 10 wickets down) before facing its full 20 overs, the full 20 overs are used in the calculation anyway — not the overs actually faced.
- Overs are counted in real balls (e.g. 19.3 overs = 19 overs and 3 balls), not as a plain decimal.
- Abandoned or no-result matches are excluded from NRR entirely.

---

## 3. Squad Composition

- **Squad size**: 13 total — **11 players bought through the Players' Auction**, plus the **Captain** (already assigned via the Captain's Auction) and the **Manager** (already assigned), both of whom are full playing members of the squad.
- **Playing XI rule**: Every one of the 13 squad members must feature in the Playing XI for a minimum of **2 matches** across the season, including the Manager and Captain. The **Manager and Captain are jointly responsible** for ensuring this rule is met through squad rotation.

---

## 4. Auction Rules

- **Purse per team**: ₹25,00,000 (full purse)
- **Player base price**: ₹50,000

### Bid increments (slab-based)
| Current bid range | Increment |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹1,00,000 | ₹5,000 |
| ₹1,00,000 – ₹2,00,000 | ₹10,000 |
| ₹2,00,000 – ₹3,00,000 | ₹20,000 |
| ₹3,00,000 – ₹4,00,000 | ₹25,000 |
| Above ₹4,00,000 | ₹50,000 |

### Purse reserve rule
A team cannot bid an amount that would leave it unable to afford the minimum remaining squad size (12 players) at base price. Once a bid would push the team below (remaining players needed × ₹50,000) in reserve, that bid is blocked.

### Timeouts
Each team gets **3 strategic timeouts** of **90 seconds** each during the auction, to be used at the manager's discretion during a live bid.

### Roll & Unsold Rules
- Players are rolled at random within their skill category (Batting / Bowling / All-Rounder [Batting] / All-Rounder [Bowling]).
- A player who goes unsold is not re-offered again within the same round, but becomes eligible again once that round completes.

## 7. Match Day Rules

- **Reporting time**: 6:15 AM
- **Toss time**: 6:20 AM
- **Match start time**: 6:30 AM
- **Playing XI submission**: 15 minutes before the toss
- **Grounds**: League and Qualifier matches at Legends Ground B / Legends Ground C. Final at AXI Cricket Ground.
- **Playing conditions**: Standard T20 rules apply — wides and no-balls as per standard; byes included, leg byes excluded; 6-over powerplay.
- **Weather/Rain**: Results are determined via DLS (Duckworth-Lewis-Stern) as computed through CricHeroes.
- **Player replacement**: At the committee's discretion.
