Net Run Rate (NRR)

NRR = (Runs scored ÷ Overs faced) − (Runs conceded ÷ Overs bowled), totalled across all matches played — not calculated per match.

  • If a team is bowled out (all 10 wickets down) before facing its full 20 overs, the full 20 overs are still used in the calculation.
  • Overs are counted in real balls — e.g. 19.3 overs means 19 overs and 3 balls, not a plain decimal.
  • Abandoned or no-result matches are excluded from NRR entirely.

Purse Reserve Rule

A team can't place a bid that would leave it unable to afford the minimum remaining squad (12 players) at base price. Once a bid would push the team below (players still needed × ₹50,000) in reserve, that bid is blocked automatically.

Timeouts

Each team gets 3 strategic timeouts of 90 seconds each during the auction, to be used at the manager's discretion during a live bid.

Roll & Unsold Rules

Players are rolled at random within their skill category (Batting / Bowling / All-Rounder [Batting] / All-Rounder [Bowling]). A player who goes unsold isn't re-offered within the same round, but becomes eligible again once that round completes.

Timing

Reporting at 6:15 AM, toss at 6:20 AM, match starts 6:30 AM. Playing XI must be submitted 15 minutes before the toss.

Grounds

League and Qualifier matches at Legends Ground B / Legends Ground C. The Final is played at AXI Cricket Ground.

Playing Conditions

Standard T20 rules apply — wides and no-balls as per standard; byes included, leg byes excluded; 6-over powerplay.

Weather / Rain

Results affected by weather are determined via DLS (Duckworth-Lewis-Stern), as computed through CricHeroes.

Player Replacement

Mid-tournament player replacements are handled at the committee's discretion.
